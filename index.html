Why I Started This Site

Most of us head off to college without ever being taught how money works. Then the first credit card offer shows up, rent is due, and we end up figuring it out as we go. I'm a college student too, and I've spent a lot of time learning about budgeting and investing on my own. This site is basically everything I wish someone had told me when I started.

Start Early, Even With a Little

You don't need a ton of money to start. What you really need is time. When you invest, your money makes money, and then that money starts making money too. That's called compound growth, and the earlier you start, the better it works. Here's an example. Two people each put in $100 a month and earn about 7% a year until they're 65. One starts at 20 and ends up with around $379,000. The other waits until 30 and ends up with around $180,000. The first person only put in $12,000 more, but ended up with about twice as much. Real returns go up and down every year, so this is just an example. Still, the point is pretty clear. Start early.
